# Skill template and checklist

## Layout

The body of a saved skill, in this order. Leave out a section that has nothing in it.

    # Weekly sales report

    ## Purpose
    One line: what this produces and for whom.

    ## Steps
    1. Concrete step: which app or site, which folder, which file name pattern.
    2. Next step, in the order it's done.
    3. The check before handing over (totals add up, the date range is last week).

    ## Rules
    1. What to confirm with the person first (anything sent, bought or deleted).
    2. What never to do.
    3. Preferences they stated for this task (format, length, tone).

    ## Hand-over
    What they get and how: a file shared in the chat, a short summary, a draft for them to send.

## Description line

- Starts with "Use when", one line, under 300 characters.
- Names the trigger the way they'd say it: "Use when Sam asks for the weekly sales report or it's Monday's report run."
- Specific enough that it can't be confused with another skill.

## Checklist before saving

- One job only.
- The steps say what to do next, in order; no background essay.
- Every app, folder, file, login and key named really exists; nothing invented.
- No secrets in the text.
- Anything that sends, posts, buys or deletes says to confirm with the person first.
- Not a copy of a built-in skill: if a built-in skill covers the task, this one only adds their own steps and preferences, and says to follow the built-in one too.
