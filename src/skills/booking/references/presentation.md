# Showing booking options

The person should see the recommendation, the tradeoff, the full cost and the next step without reading a wall of text.

## Shape

1. **One sentence of recommendation:** the best option and the deciding reason, with any caveat in a short clause.
2. **One compact comparison** in a Markdown table, three to five distinct options at most.
3. **One decision:** what you need from them next.

Don't repeat the table's prices, times or terms in prose. Secondary facts go in a short labelled line after they pick an
option or ask. With only one exact match, skip the table and use a few labelled lines.

## Table rules

- Stable option labels (A, B, C) used in every follow-up.
- Six columns or fewer; combine related facts in one cell.
- The full total for the whole party or stay, never a per-person or per-night teaser alone.
- The same fields in the same order for every option.
- Bold only the recommended option's label and the most important changed fact.
- A fact the site didn't give: "Not stated". Never drop a field whose absence could change the decision.
- Local dates and times (airport, hotel, venue, restaurant), with the year when it could be unclear.
- No internal ids, tool names or raw output. Link each option's name or label to its live page.

**Before you send, check it once:** every number in the table is from a page you read in this task; each total is for the
whole party (a per-person fare shown as per person, or multiplied out and said so); and any "fastest", "cheapest" or
"recommended because" claim matches the numbers in your own table (compare the durations and totals, don't recall them).

## Flights

| Option | Route and local times | Duration | Stops and layovers | Flight and cabin | Full total |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| **A (recommended)** | SFO 8:10 AM → JFK 4:45 PM | 5h 35m | Nonstop | UA 123 · Economy | $642 round trip, 2 adults |
| B (cheapest) | SFO 7:00 AM → DEN 10:25 AM → JFK 4:10 PM | 6h 10m | 1 stop · DEN 1h 05m | UA 456/789 · Economy | $511 round trip, 2 adults |

One row per complete itinerary; a return trip keeps both directions in the same row. Every row shows: full party price and
currency, one-way or round trip; each leg's local times and airports; each leg's duration; nonstop or the exact number of
stops; every connection airport and layover; airline and flight numbers (operating airline when different); cabin and fare
brand when stated. Shared bag or fare-rule notes go in one short "Details:" line under the table.

## Hotels

| Option | Hotel and exact room | Location | Full stay total | Terms and benefits | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

Exact room and bed, dates, total with mandatory fees, amount due at the property, cancellation deadline, and loyalty
benefits that matter. When location matters, a real travel time to the named place rather than a vague area claim, and a map
link per hotel.

## Restaurants

| Option | Restaurant | Time and seating | Cuisine and price | Location | Deposit or cancellation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Exact available time, seating, cuisine, price level, address or travel time, and any deposit, minimum spend, service charge,
cancellation or no-show fee. The recommendation names the one reason (taste, place, time or value) the first option wins.

## Event tickets

| Option | Event and local time | Exact seats | Primary or resale | Delivered total | Important terms |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|

Ticket count, section, row, seats when assigned, view restrictions, primary or resale, fees, delivery method and hold
expiry. Every option you offer needs a working link to that exact listing; drop one you can't link.

## Final review

After they choose, don't show another big comparison. Use a two-column table at the commitment point:

| Review | Selected terms |
|---|---|
| Item | Exact itinerary, property and room, table, or seats |
| Date and time | Local date and time |
| Total | Full amount and currency, due now and due later |
| Applied value | Credits, points, certificates or benefits used |
| Payment | Link (or "you pay at the property", "you'll enter your card") |
| Terms | Cancellation, refund, change or no-show terms |

End with one plain question asking for their OK on exactly these terms. When Link will ask them to approve the same total, the
Link approval is the payment confirmation; your question confirms the booking terms.

## After it's booked

A short receipt:

- **Status:** Confirmed, Held, Waitlisted or Blocked
- **Confirmation:** the reference
- **When and where:** local date, time and place
- **Total:** paid or committed
- **Next:** the next deadline or step, only when there is one

Never show full payment, passport, traveller or loyalty numbers.
