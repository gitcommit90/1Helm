# From plan to live check

This keeps a travel decision intact while you swap planning evidence for live prices and terms. It's your own working
state, not a form to show the person.

## Who does the live part

Read the booking skill (`read_skill` with slug booking) and its reference for the kind of thing: flights, hotels,
restaurants, tickets. Rail, transfers, rental cars and activities follow the booking skill's general steps on the
operator's or a reputable seller's own site. Don't make the person repeat the brief.

## Carry forward

- the trip posture (planning only, or ready to book) and any "finish planning first" instruction;
- what they want and which candidate they selected, if any, with its decision state;
- for a stay, event, activity, restaurant or transfer: the exact named item, place, local date and time or window, length,
  party, children's ages or access needs, and what depends on it;
- traveller counts and children's ages for pricing, and the cabin;
- every flight segment's airports, local times, marketing and operating flight numbers;
- one-ticket, self-transfer, airport-change, overnight and connection limits;
- how flexible they said they are on dates, times, airports, airlines, cabin, route and ticket type;
- budget, bags, seats, access, loyalty, and refund or change needs;
- each planning source with its link and when you checked, and any indicative price kept apart from live prices.

Don't carry a rejected item forward, and never invent a traveller, airport, date, cabin or flexibility to make a search run.

## Matching the live result

For a non-flight item, match the exact provider or venue, place, local date and time, party or quantity, variant and any
access or age requirement before comparing price and terms. For flights, compare every returned segment in order: same
airports, same scheduled local times and dates, same marketing and operating flights (when both are stated), same order,
same cabin on every segment. A fact missing from the live result makes the match unverified, not exact: check the airline's
site or say so; don't fill it from the planning source.

A matching itinerary doesn't mean a matching fare: the live fare brand, bags, ticket structure, total, refundability,
changeability and penalties are a new offer.

Sort the result into one of four:

- **exact match:** compare the live terms with the plan;
- **within the flexibility they gave:** you may recommend it, saying what differs;
- **material mismatch:** route, schedule, cabin, ticket type, price or condition moved outside their limits: the choice goes
  back to them;
- **not found on this site:** not proof it doesn't exist anywhere.

Never quietly fold the last two into the selected candidate.

## Flexibility, honestly

When flexibility matters, put a good cheaper offer next to a genuinely refundable or changeable one, and keep apart:
refundable / non-refundable / not stated; refund to card versus credit; changeable versus refundable; change or cancel fees
(or not stated); paying the fare difference after a change; deadlines. An unknown condition is neither strict nor flexible:
say "not stated".

## Back to the plan, or on to booking

In **planning only**, live prices and availability are research: compare the exact options, then stop. No checkout, no
traveller or payment details, no "which one shall I book?". Write the result back into the plan item (evidence and booking
state change; the decision doesn't) and ask the next planning question. When dates depend on both the stay and the
transport, compare both before recommending either be bought.

Before moving a multi-part trip to **ready to book**, show the current shape and what's still open, and offer the choice:
keep planning, or start booking now. Their choice changes the posture; a live search or a favourite they point to doesn't.
After they pick a flight while still planning, offer "keep it in the plan and book later" or "book it now".

If nothing exact or within their flexibility is live, come back with the specific mismatch and the strongest real
alternatives, keeping their settled decisions. An alternative outside their limits is a new proposed item; it doesn't
replace the selected one or revive a rejected one.

Once they're ready to book and have chosen a live option, the booking skill owns the price check, the approval, the
purchase and the confirmation. Choosing in the plan is never approval to buy.
