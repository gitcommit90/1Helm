12 The Process

how it works

01
Discover
Talk to users. Read the data. Surface what's actually true vs. what the team assumes.
→
02
Define
Narrow to one problem worth solving. Write it as a testable hypothesis, not a feature request.
→
03
Design
Build the simplest thing that could prove or disprove the hypothesis. Skip the polish.
→
04
Deploy
Ship to real users. Measure behavior, not opinions. Repeat from step one with new data.
