Three rules we're keeping

Pinned to the wall above every desk. We refer back to them when a decision feels too big to make from the seat we're in.
Rule · 01

Write the
real sentence

If a customer wouldn't read the email, the email is not the work. Plain words, signed by a person, sent on a Tuesday.

— write it by hand first.
Rule · 02

Earn the
second look

Every interaction in the first 72 hours is doing four times the work of one in week three. Spend accordingly.

no autoresponder, ever.
Rule · 03

Keep the
handwriting

The system is allowed to grow, but the voice on the other end stays small enough to know who you wrote to last week.

200 names, max.
