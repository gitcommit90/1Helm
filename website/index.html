01The how

Every account you have. One fabric. Zero ceremony.

Connect multiple ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and xAI accounts by OAuth; add OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, Cloudflare, GLM, or any custom endpoint by key. Enable exact models, compose named routes, and every resident rides the fabric through a private local gateway. This is a live simulation — click an account to knock it offline.

    Routes, not roulette

    A route is a named, ordered policy — fallback tries the next healthy account; round-robin spreads load. Disabled or exhausted accounts are excluded from attempts and from logs. One dead account never blocks a healthy one serving the same model.

    Identity outlives the route

    Changing a model or route never replaces a resident or discards its computer, memory, skills, files, obligations, or thread history. Agents authenticate with a private internal credential — revoke every external key and the crew stays online.

    Your gateway, for everything

    The same fabric exposes an authenticated OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible /v1 endpoint. Point external tools at your own router: your accounts, your quotas, your logs, your machine.

    02The details

    One channel. One world.

    Create a channel for anything that deserves an owner — a product, an inbox, a client, a household. 1Helm provisions a complete world around it. Hover the parts.

    AOne permanent resident

    The employee identity. It survives threads, restarts, and even model changes.

    BOne private Linux computer

    A real Apple container machine VM with the Mac home directory unmounted. Agent tools and your Terminal share the same persistent /workspace.

    CMemory with provenance

    Curated decisions, corrections, preferences, and outcomes — not a transcript dump — plus an isolated Mnemosyne store for long-term recall.

    DA serious skill arsenal

    Relevant playbooks activate automatically. Routine tool use is not an approval ritual.

    EDurable obligations

    Follow-ups, timers, and recurring workflows with due times and retry state. They can wake the computer back up.

    FThreads & files

    Durable work sessions with status, evidence, and a Board view. Files live in the same computer the work happens in.

    03For the skeptics

    The handoff you never have to make.

    Most agent products make you the router: copy context here, grant permission there, paste the result back. In 1Helm the resident calls Skipper itself — and the thread comes back on its own.

    01

    The resident works

    Inspects, installs, edits, runs, retries — autonomously, inside its own computer.

    02

    It hits the boundary

    Needs the host, a credential, or another channel's resident. Work it can't — and shouldn't — do alone.

    03

    It calls Skipper. Not you.

    Skipper performs the boundary operation with host-authorized provenance.

    04

    The thread returns automatically

    Skipper hands the exact same thread back to the same resident. No relay, no copy-paste.

    05

    The resident verifies end-to-end

    The original outcome gets checked and evidenced — not assumed.
